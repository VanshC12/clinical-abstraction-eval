DISCHARGE SUMMARY
Patient: 28 y/o female. Brought to ED by boyfriend after intentional insulin overdose in the setting of a suicide attempt.
ED course: Arrival glucose 22 mg/dL, obtunded. D50 x2 given immediately, then started on D10 infusion. Mental status improved. Psychiatry consulted in the ED.
Inpatient course: Admitted to medicine for glucose monitoring given long-acting insulin ingestion. D10 infusion continued for 36 hours with q1h then q2h glucose checks. All values after the initial ED value remained above 70 mg/dL. No insulin or any other antidiabetic medication was administered by the hospital at any time during this admission.
Psychiatry followed daily. Patient transferred to inpatient behavioral health unit on HD3 on a voluntary basis.
Crisis resources and safety plan documented.
