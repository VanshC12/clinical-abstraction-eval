PSYCHIATRIC INPATIENT DISCHARGE SUMMARY
Patient: 31 y/o male. Admitted voluntarily for major depressive disorder with suicidal ideation.
PMH: MDD, generalized anxiety disorder. No diabetes mellitus. No endocrine history.
Course: Started on sertraline 50 mg daily, titrated to 100 mg. Group and individual therapy daily. Suicidal ideation resolved by day 5 with no plan or intent at discharge. Safety plan completed with the patient and his sister.
Medications during stay: sertraline, hydroxyzine PRN, melatonin.
No antidiabetic medications administered. Admission metabolic panel glucose 88 mg/dL; no further glucose monitoring was clinically indicated or performed.
Discharged day 7 with outpatient psychiatry appointment in 5 days and therapy weekly.
