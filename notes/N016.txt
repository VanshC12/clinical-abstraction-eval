DISCHARGE SUMMARY
Patient: 86 y/o female, nursing home resident. Admitted with altered mental status.
PMH: Type 2 DM on glipizide 10 mg daily, dementia, CKD stage 3.
Hospital course: Home glipizide was continued on admission. Workup for AMS included head CT (negative), UA (negative), CXR (negative).
On HD1 at 1830 the patient became progressively more somnolent. Point-of-care glucose 27 mg/dL. D50 administered with improvement in alertness. Given sulfonylurea half-life and renal impairment, patient was placed on a D10 infusion for 24 hours with q2h glucose checks. Two additional values below 60 occurred during that window (54, 58) but none below 40.
Glipizide discontinued permanently. Pharmacy consulted.
Discharged back to facility HD4 on diet-controlled management.
