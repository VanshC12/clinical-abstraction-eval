DISCHARGE SUMMARY
34 y/o male, type 1 diabetes, admitted with acute pancreatitis.
Course: NPO on admission with IV fluids and pain control. Lipase peaked at 1840. Diet advanced slowly from HD3.
Insulin management: While NPO, patient was placed on correctional insulin aspart sliding scale q4h plus glargine 22 units nightly (home dose continued unchanged).
Event log: HD2 0200 - accucheck 31 mg/dL. Patient diaphoretic, tachycardic, difficult to arouse. Rapid response called. D50 x1 given, glucose 148 at 0230. The preceding correctional aspart dose of 6 units had been administered at 2200 HD1 while the patient was still NPO.
Glargine reduced to 14 units. Sliding scale changed to NPO scale. No further events.
Discharged HD6 tolerating low-fat diet.
