DISCHARGE SUMMARY
Patient: 83 y/o female. Admitted for dehydration and failure to thrive.
PMH: Type 2 DM on glyburide 5 mg BID, dementia, depression.
Course: IV hydration. Nutrition consulted. Patient with markedly poor oral intake, eating less than 25 percent of meals.
Diabetes: Home glyburide continued on admission at full dose.
Event: HD2 at 1145 patient noted to be lethargic and diaphoretic by the dietary aide. Nursing accucheck 34 mg/dL. D50 administered, repeat glucose 121 at 1210. Patient returned to baseline mental status.
Glyburide discontinued. Endocrine and pharmacy both documented that sulfonylureas are inappropriate in this patient given age, dementia, and unreliable intake.
Discharged HD5 on diet-controlled management with home health aide.
